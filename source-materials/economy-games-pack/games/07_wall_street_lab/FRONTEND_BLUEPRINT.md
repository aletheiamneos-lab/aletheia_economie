# FRONTEND BLUEPRINT — Wall Street Lab
Main scene: fictional trading floor and portfolio desk.

Interactive elements:
- share/bond cards;
- price slider;
- coupon/dividend calculator;
- maturity timeline;
- primary/secondary market switch;
- drag assets into portfolio;
- compare yields;
- buy/sell simulation;
- reveal formula;
- restart.

Visual:
coupon payments travel from issuer to bondholder; dividends animate separately; title ownership/creditor status is shown with distinct iconography.
