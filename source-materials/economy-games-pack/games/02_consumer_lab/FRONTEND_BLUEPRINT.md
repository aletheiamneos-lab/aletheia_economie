# FRONTEND BLUEPRINT — Consumer Lab
Main scene: product shelf + basket + budget + satisfaction.

Interactive elements:
- drag product to basket;
- +/- quantity controls;
- remove item;
- budget meter;
- total utility meter;
- reveal marginal utility after first attempt;
- UT/Umg chart;
- Umg/P comparison cards;
- income-change animation;
- price-change animation;
- compare current vs previous basket;
- `Optimizează` available only after attempt;
- restart.

Visual behavior:
each extra unit becomes visually less intense as marginal utility falls.
Negative marginal utility visibly lowers the satisfaction meter.
