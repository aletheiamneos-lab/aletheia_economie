# FRONTEND BLUEPRINT — President
Main scene: Econia city + presidential decision room.

Interactive elements:
- 20 decision cards;
- three-choice decision panel;
- cabinet advisers with short arguments;
- 9 live indicators;
- four-year timeline;
- event cards;
- trend charts;
- delayed-effect badges;
- policy history;
- compare beginning vs current mandate;
- final report;
- replay mandate with same seed;
- restart.

Visual:
construction cranes for public investment, price labels for inflation, factory activity for growth, crowds/jobs for unemployment, containers for trade, government plaza for approval.

Critical rule:
do not show hidden numerical effects before the player chooses.
