# FRONTEND BLUEPRINT — Central Bank
Main scene: monetary-policy command center.

Interactive elements:
- policy-rate dial;
- +/- 0.5 pp controls;
- quarter simulation button;
- inflation/growth/unemployment monitors;
- credit-flow visualization;
- policy-lag timeline;
- stability gauge;
- chart history;
- compare to previous quarter;
- scenario replay;
- restart.

Animation:
rate dial rotates, credit pipes narrow/widen, investment buildings pulse, indicators transition gradually rather than jump.
